About me

Understanding Earth's Climate System

I am an atmospheric scientist and an Assistant Research Scientist at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC), working at NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center.

My research focuses on understanding clouds and their role in Earth's climate system, particularly how cloud properties and their radiative effects influence Earth's energy budget and its variability. Using satellite observations, I investigate cloud regimes, cloud–radiation interactions, and cloud feedbacks, with a growing interest in understanding the contributions of cloud systems to recent changes in Earth's energy imbalance.

Outside of research, I enjoy exploring different hobbies, often finding myself drawn to a particular activity for a while before moving on to something else. These days, my interests include running, golfing, reading, and knitting.
